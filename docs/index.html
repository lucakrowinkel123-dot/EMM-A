EMM-A 0.9.47B

The Pulsar Legacy client plugin requires a working SDX2 targeted lidar on the carrier. Its range must touch the asteroid's bounding box for EMM-A to scan the whole asteroid. Use the five 0.9.46B programmable-block scripts to fly the carrier and miner.
